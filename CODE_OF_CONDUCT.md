# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

MindQuest welcomes constructive participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels. We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free environment.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Critique ideas and implementation, not people.
- Provide clear, actionable technical feedback.
- Respect privacy, intellectual property, and the project's proprietary licence.
- Keep discussions relevant and suitable for a broad-age audience.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, doxxing, sexualized harassment, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and may restrict participation where necessary to protect contributors and users. Reports will be reviewed as fairly and confidentially as practical.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to **cyber.litigation.advisor@gmail.com**. Include relevant links and context. Do not publish sensitive personal information in a public issue.

## Scope

This Code applies in project repositories, issue and pull-request discussions, and other spaces when an individual is representing or participating in the MindQuest project.
