YOUR DATA, YOUR DEVICE

How MindQuest handles your data?

MindQuest is designed to work offline without an account, advertising, analytics SDK, or gameplay backend. Your language choices, preferences and best scores are stored locally on this device when storage is available.

The app does not ask for personal profile details and is not designed to transmit gameplay preferences or scores to the developer. Distribution platforms may process information under their own privacy terms.

Clear app storage or uninstall the app to remove locally stored preferences and scores, subject to operating-system behaviour.

For the complete policy, see Privacy Policy in the repository.

ACCESS FOR EVERY MIND

Accessibility Statement

MindQuest aims to provide an inclusive experience. The development target is WCAG 2.2 Level AA where applicable to the bundled web interface. This is a target, not a verified conformance claim or independent certification.

Current practices include semantic controls, keyboard focus management, live status announcements, language metadata, responsive layouts, reduced-motion styling and forced-colors support. Further manual testing with TalkBack, screen readers, keyboard-only navigation, text scaling and contrast checks remains necessary.

W3C conformance logos are intentionally withheld until the applicable pages have been evaluated and a valid conformance claim can be supported. See W3C logo usage guidance.

For the complete statement, see Accessibility Statement in the repository.

THE QUICK GUIDE

How to play

Play a round

Choose a region, question language, category and difficulty in Settings, then start a round. Each round contains up to the selected number of multiple-choice questions.

Scoring and results

Choose one answer per question. Correct answers earn one point. Your results show your score, accuracy, the correct answers and explanations.

Categories and difficulty

Explore Geography, History, Science or Culture. Choose Easy, Medium or Hard, or leave the filters open to mix the available questions.

Three independent choices

Interface language controls menus; question region selects the knowledge set; question language controls question wording. Any supported combination is valid.

Free, private and offline

MindQuest is free to play, works offline and needs no account. Settings and best scores are stored on this device when browser storage is available; no ads or paid services are used.
