YOUR DATA, YOUR DEVICE

How MindQuest Handles Your Data?

Effective date: 18 November 2026
Application: MindQuest (Мозговой вызов)
Developer: Er. Tapan Jatakia (C. Eng.)

1. Overview

MindQuest is designed as a free, offline-first puzzle and trivia application. The current product design does not require an account, advertising identifier, analytics SDK, telemetry service, cloud profile, or application backend.

This policy describes the intended behaviour of the application build distributed with this document. If a future release adds a data-processing feature, this policy and the in-app disclosures must be reviewed before that feature is enabled.

2. Information handled by the application

MindQuest stores gameplay preferences and best-score records locally on the device when platform storage is available. These may include:

  • Interface language;
  • Question-bank region and question language;
  • Selected category, difficulty and round length;
  • Best-score records associated with game settings.

The game does not ask players to provide their name, postal address, phone number, account credentials, contacts, precise location, photographs, microphone recordings, or payment information.

The application is not designed to transmit these preferences or scores to the developer. Local storage can be cleared by uninstalling the application or using the device's application-storage controls; exact behaviour depends on the operating system and backup settings.

3. Network access and third parties

The Android application is designed to load bundled application files and question content locally. It does not require an internet connection for ordinary gameplay and does not intentionally integrate advertising networks, analytics providers, tracking SDKs, social login, or remote game APIs.

The distribution platform (for example, RuStore or GitHub) may independently process information under its own privacy terms when a user visits its store, downloads the app, or uses its services. Such platform processing is outside the application's local gameplay storage.

4. Children and families

MindQuest is intended to be suitable for a broad audience. It does not intentionally collect personal information from children or other players. Parents and guardians should supervise device-level permissions, backups, and app installation in accordance with their family's needs.

5. Data retention and security

Preferences and scores remain in application storage until changed, cleared, or removed with the app data/uninstallation, subject to operating-system behaviour. The application uses local storage for game functionality; users should protect access to their devices and keep their operating systems updated.

No software can guarantee absolute security. If a future release changes the data-handling model, the relevant retention, transfer, security and user-control details will be documented before release.

6. Your choices and rights

You can change or reset in-app settings where those controls are provided and can clear application data through your device settings. Depending on your location, privacy laws may provide additional rights. Because the current application design does not send gameplay preferences or scores to the developer, the developer ordinarily does not hold a remote copy of those local records to retrieve, export, or delete.

For questions, concerns, or a privacy request, contact the developer using the details below.

7. Changes to this policy

The policy version and effective date will be updated when material changes are made. The current policy is available in full within the app and in the repository.

ACCESS FOR EVERY MIND

Accessibility Statement

Effective date: 18 November 2026
Application: MindQuest (Мозговой вызов)

Commitment

MindQuest aims to provide an inclusive puzzle and trivia experience across supported devices, input methods, languages, and accessibility preferences. Accessibility is an ongoing engineering and review activity; this statement does not represent an independent certification.

Standards and conformance responsibility

MindQuest is being developed with the objective of conforming to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA, where applicable to its bundled web interface. The Android experience also requires platform-level testing with Android accessibility services.

Responsibility for conformance: The application owner, Er. Tapan Jatakia, is responsible for completing the remaining accessibility work, conducting the conformance assessment, and ensuring that MindQuest conforms to WCAG 2.2 Level AA before making a verified conformance claim. This includes reviewing the exact release build, testing supported browsers and Android devices, validating keyboard and assistive-technology operation, and remediating identified barriers.

The W3C WCAG 2.2 Level AA conformance logo links to the official explanation of the conformance requirements. Its display represents the application owner’s conformance declaration; it is not a W3C audit, endorsement, or independent certification. The application owner is responsible for ensuring that the exact version presented as conforming has been evaluated against all applicable WCAG 2.2 Level AA success criteria and conformance requirements, including manual review and assistive-technology testing.

Accessibility features and design practices

The following engineering features and automated browser checks are implemented:

  • Semantic headings, labels, buttons, and form controls;
  • Keyboard focus visibility and programmatic focus movement between views;
  • Status announcements for progress, answer feedback, and results;
  • Language metadata for localized questions and answer choices;
  • Responsive layouts and support for narrow screens;
  • Reduced-motion styling;
  • Forced-colors/high-contrast styling;
  • Localized interface and question content with independent language choices.

The presence of source-level support does not guarantee that every combination works with every assistive technology or device configuration.

Known limitations and verification

Automated browser regression currently covers keyboard navigation and focus transitions, accessible names and ARIA references, live-region semantics, language metadata, large-text/high-contrast/reduced-motion behaviour at compact widths, and contrast measurements for ten themes across twelve viewport profiles. These results are limited to the tested browser configurations and do not establish conformance.

The remaining acceptance work for this release is physical Android verification with TalkBack and other supported screen readers, switch-access operation, and device-specific focus, text-scaling, contrast and layout behaviour. Those checks must be recorded against the exact candidate build.

Please report barriers with the device model, Android/browser version, assistive technology, steps to reproduce, and (if possible) a description of the affected screen. Do not include sensitive personal information in a report.

Accessibility feedback is welcome. The project will assess reported barriers, prioritize issues by impact, and document material changes. Users may contact the project accessibility feedback contact using the details below.

THE QUICK GUIDE

How to play

Play a round

Choose a region, question language, category and difficulty in Settings, then start a round. Each round contains up to the selected number of multiple-choice questions.

Scoring and results

Choose one answer per question. Correct answers earn one point. Your results show your score, accuracy, the correct answers and explanations.

Categories and difficulty

Explore Geography, History, Science or Culture. Choose Easy, Medium or Hard, or leave the filters open to mix the available questions.

Three independent choices

Interface language controls menus; question region selects the knowledge set; question language controls question wording. Any supported combination is valid.

Free, private and offline

MindQuest is free to play, works offline and needs no account. Settings and best scores are stored on this device when browser storage is available; no ads or paid services are used.
