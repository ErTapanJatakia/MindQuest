# Spain and Peru question-bank expansion — combined batches 03–04

## Scope and release boundary

This research register plans 40 additional original questions (20 Spain, 20 Peru), each with five explicit locale records: en-IN, en-US, ru-RU, es-ES and es-PE. The resulting 200 localized records remain staged and must not be loaded by the application until the full 50-question banks and all editorial gates pass.

All translations will be assistant-prepared drafts. Native-speaker review, factual review and independent answer-key review remain mandatory.
