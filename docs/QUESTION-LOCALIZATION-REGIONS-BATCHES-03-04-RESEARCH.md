# Spain and Peru question-bank expansion — combined batches 03–04

## Scope and release boundary

This research register plans 40 additional original questions (20 Spain, 20 Peru), each with five explicit locale records: en-IN, en-US, ru-RU, es-ES and es-PE. The resulting 200 localized records remain staged and must not be loaded by the application until the full 50-question banks and all editorial gates pass.

All translations will be assistant-prepared drafts. Native-speaker review, factual review and independent answer-key review remain mandatory.

## Quotas

Each of batches 03 and 04 contains 10 questions per region.

| Batch | Geography | History | Science | Culture | Easy | Medium | Hard |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 03, per region | 2 | 3 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 3 |
| 04, per region | 3 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 3 |
| Combined 03–04, per region | 5 | 5 | 4 | 6 | 6 | 8 | 6 |

After batches 01–04, each region will have 40 staged questions with cumulative category counts Geography 11, History 10, Science 9, Culture 10 and difficulty counts easy 12, medium 16, hard 12. The final batch 05 will supply the remaining 10 questions per region and meet the exact target quotas.

## Editorial cautions

- Confirm each exact claim and wording against the cited source before finalising question text.
- Avoid duplicate subject matter with batches 01–02 (for example, do not repeat Nasca Lines, Chan Chan, Titicaca, Colca, the Guadalquivir, the Meseta, Magellan, Isaac Peral, or the Ayacucho battle).
- Where a source URL is a portal rather than a specific item, replace it with a direct institutional page before the content batch is marked fact-checked.
- Ensure question wording has one unambiguous answer and all four distractors are plausible but clearly incorrect.
- Native-speaker review is required for all five locales, with particular attention to es-ES versus es-PE vocabulary and naturalness.
